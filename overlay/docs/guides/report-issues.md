---
title: Reporting Problems
---

**Staff:** call the administrator and say what you were doing and what you saw. Do not restart repeatedly or try to fix it yourself.

**Administrators:** collect these before reporting:

```bash
sudo bootc status
ujust logs-last-boot
```

Report NECD-specific problems on the [NECD OS issue tracker](https://github.com/Northeast-Childrens-Dentistry/necdos/issues). Problems that also happen on stock Aurora belong on the [Aurora tracker](https://github.com/ublue-os/aurora/issues).
